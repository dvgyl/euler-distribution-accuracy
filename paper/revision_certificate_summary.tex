\section{Certification and target-path error}
\label{sec:certificate-summary}

The local projected-defect formula is established in recent work
\citep{obzherin2026density,obzherin2026finite}.  In the present
Euclidean setting, Corollary~\ref{cor:sharp-local-residual} proves it
under the smoothness, Lipschitz and second-moment assumptions used here,
without a density or a regular projected solenoidal flow:
\[
 \lim_{h\downarrow0}h^{-2}
 W_2((\operatorname{Id}+hu_t)_\#p_t,p_{t+h})
 =\tfrac12\varepsilon_t.
\]
The new global coefficient retains cancellation between transported
defects; a sum of local norms alone cannot identify that coefficient.

For finite error control, Theorem~\ref{thm:projected-residual} gives
explicit constants $D_H=O(1)$ and $B_H=1+O(H)$ with
\begin{equation}
 \max_k W_2(\widehat p_k,p_{t_k})
 \le e^{LT}\left(D_H\sum_jh_j^3+
                    \frac{B_H}{2}\sum_jh_j^2\varepsilon_{t_j}\right).
 \label{eq:certificate-summary}
\end{equation}
Its proof follows the full acceleration flow and controls its change of
law, rather than assuming that the projected solenoidal component has a
regular flow.  Node certificates $\varepsilon_{t_j}\le K H$ give
second-order accuracy if the remaining constants are uniform.
The quantitative odd-dimensional obstruction makes this residual
requirement impossible under a fixed Lipschitz budget on its stated
Gaussian paths.

Certification requires an upper bound.  For
$q\in\mathcal G_p$ and $w\in\mathcal G_p^\perp$, orthogonality gives
\begin{align*}
 \max\{0,2\langle a,q\rangle-\|q\|^2\}
 &\le\|\Pi_pa\|^2\le\|a-w\|^2,\\
 \|a-w\|^2-2\langle a,q\rangle+\|q\|^2
 &=\|a-w-q\|^2.
\end{align*}
The complete statements include weighted-Poisson, approximation-error,
compact gauge-search, and time-grid certificates.  A finite gradient
projection norm is a lower bound and cannot by itself certify small
residual.

If $u$ is integrated while a different field $v$ generates the intended
target $p_t$, Theorem~\ref{thm:target-path-bound} adds initialization
error and
$\int_0^T\|\Pi_{p_t}(u_t-v_t)\|_2\,\dd t$ to
\eqref{eq:certificate-summary}, under its explicit mixed-jerk bound.
Population excess flow-matching regression loss controls this latter
term by Cauchy--Schwarz.  It does not alone imply the required regularity
or eliminate discretization error.  All proofs and precise assumptions
are retained in the supplement.
