\section{A nonradial positive construction}
\label{sec:positive-summary}

The obstruction concerns a specified class of paths.  Nontrivial
acceleration-preserving paths do exist.  A useful sufficient construction
starts from a reference law $\rho$, a field $b$ preserving $\rho$, and
smooth diffeomorphisms satisfying
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 F_{tt}(z)&=DF_t(z)b(z),\\
 p_t&=(F_t)_\#\rho,\qquad u_t(F_tz)=F_t'(z).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:reference-summary}
\end{equation}
Then $\acc_t(F_tz)=DF_t(z)b(z)$ and
$\int\nabla\phi\cdot\acc_t\,\dd p_t
=\int\nabla(\phi\circ F_t)\cdot b\,\dd\rho=0$.

Identify $\R^2$ with $\mathbb C$, take $a,\epsilon>0$ and
$\rho=\mathcal N(0,I_2)$, and define
\begin{equation}
 F_t(z)=e^{(1+i)at}z+\epsilon e^{(1+i)a\sqrt2t}z^2e^{-|z|^2}.
 \label{eq:positive-summary-map}
\end{equation}
If $(4\epsilon/e)e^{a(\sqrt2-1)T}<1$, these maps are uniformly
bi-Lipschitz smooth diffeomorphisms and satisfy
$F_{tt}=2a^2DF_tJz$.  The complete construction theorem and proof
(Theorem~\ref{thm:harmonic-construction}) verify all the global
regularity and moment assumptions and exclude every affine-radial and
affine-product representation of the target laws.  Replacing the second
time coefficient by $\epsilon\sinh((1+i)a\sqrt2t)$ gives a Gaussian
starting law under the stated smallness condition
(Corollary~\ref{cor:gaussian-start}).  These shape-changing paths are not
scalar dilations, so there is no conflict with the rigidity theorem.

The path-preserving family
\[
 u_{\omega,t}(F_tz)=F_t'(z)+(\omega-a)DF_t(z)Jz
\]
admits an exact continuum upper certificate for
$\|\Pi_{p_t}\acc_{u_\omega,t}\|$.  Minimizing over a solenoidal
acceleration comparison field eliminates one scalar analytically and
leaves a coercive quartic in $\omega$.  Evaluating all its real cubic
stationary points recovers the unique zero $\omega=a$; a positive local
minimum demonstrates the limitation of local optimization
(Proposition~\ref{prop:harmonic-search}).  This is a global recovery
guarantee within the specified family.

For $u_a$, an explicit phase coupling satisfies
\[
 W_2(\widehat p_N,p_T)
 \le\|\widehat X_N-F_T(e^{-ia^2Th}Z)\|_2=O(h^2),
\]
while the common-start particle error divided by $h$ tends to the
strictly positive constant
$a^2T\|DF_T(Z)JZ\|_2$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:harmonic-coupling}).
The construction provides a positive counterpart to the global
characterization and nonlinear obstruction; it is not an inverse solver
for arbitrary supplied density paths.
